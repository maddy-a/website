### 🧠 About Me
I build intelligent, scalable systems where **AI meets infrastructure economics**.  
- As **Chief Architect at ZeroGPU**, I’m designing a distributed GPU platform that democratizes inference at near-zero cost using pre-trained SLM's that are capable of doing specific tasks.  
- At **AI20Labs**, Shaping the future of lightweight agentic architectures, RAG infra, and autonomous data pipelines. 
- At **Replay**, I co-founded a blockchain-based media protocol rewarding creators and viewers via tokenized micropayments.   
- At **B20Labs**, worked on high-traﬃc ad server supporting stitched ad pods and millions of daily impressions with tracking. Also worked on delivering a live-streaming platform with automated EPG and server-side ad insertion; content scheduling & analytics. 
- At **GoPro**, helped scale and roll out global subscriptions platform from 0-2M+ subscribers.  

Started my career at **Walmart Labs**, optimizing backend systems and contributed to several **Open Source** and hold three patents in media streaming and tracking.
